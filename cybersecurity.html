Cybersecurity — Network Microsegmentation

Stop lateral movement cold — automated Zero Trust network segmentation powered by Zero Networks, deployed in days without re-architecting your network

The most damaging cyberattacks don't just breach the perimeter — they move laterally across your network, escalating privileges and reaching critical systems before anyone notices. Netability partners with Zero Networks to deploy automated microsegmentation and identity-based access controls that contain threats the moment they enter, without disrupting legitimate business operations.

Automated Microsegmentation

Zero Networks automatically maps all network communications and applies granular segmentation — isolating every workload so a breach cannot spread to adjacent systems.

Identity-Based Access Control

Access policies tied to verified user identity — not IP address. Compromised machines cannot access resources the user is not authorised for, even inside the network.

Lateral Movement Prevention

Even with compromised credentials, microsegmentation prevents traversal to adjacent systems — containing ransomware and insider threats to the initial foothold.

Zero-Touch Agentless Deployment

Agentless architecture deploys full microsegmentation in days — not months. No network re-architecture required.

Continuous Network Visibility

Real-time activity maps showing exactly who connects to what, across every asset — essential for security operations and compliance audit evidence.

MFA for All Network Access

Enforce multi-factor authentication at the network layer for every privileged connection — eliminating pass-the-hash and credential reuse attacks.
