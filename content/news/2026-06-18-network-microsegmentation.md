Traditional perimeter security assumes threats come from outside. But once an attacker is inside your network, a flat network lets them move laterally to critical systems within minutes.

We've partnered with Zero Networks to deploy automated, identity-based microsegmentation — isolating every device so a breach in one place can't spread. Deployment takes days, not months.

- Automatic policy generation from observed traffic
- Identity-based access control, MFA-enforced
- No agents, no network redesign
