The Monetary Authority of Singapore amended Notice FAA-N16 on 29 December 2025, tightening requirements for the recording and retention of client communications. Licensed and exempt financial advisers now face clearer obligations around mobile call capture and audit trails.

Our compliance team has broken down the practical steps — from Tier 1 mobile recording to Microsoft Teams capture — to help your firm stay ahead of the requirements.
