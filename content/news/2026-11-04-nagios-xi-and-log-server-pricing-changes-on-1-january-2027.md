Nagios has announced that pricing for Nagios XI and Nagios Log Server licences will increase on 1 January 2027.

Nagios says the change funds continued development across the platform, including expanded platform support, further dashboard work and deeper integration between its products. Recent releases point the same way. The 2026R2 cycle brought a Wizard Builder and expanded role-based access to XI, disk usage alerting to Log Server, and a full rebuild of Fusion.

For anyone already running Nagios, or planning to, the practical question is timing.

**If you have a renewal due in the first half of 2027,** it may be worth renewing before the end of December.

**If you are planning to expand,** whether by adding hosts to XI, adding nodes to Log Server, or bringing Network Analyzer or Fusion into an existing estate, placing that order this year avoids the new pricing.

**If you are evaluating Nagios for the first time,** the next eight weeks are a good window to scope it properly and decide before the change.
