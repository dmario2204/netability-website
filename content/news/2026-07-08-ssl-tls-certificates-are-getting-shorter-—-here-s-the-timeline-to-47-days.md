The industry is phasing out long-lived SSL/TLS certificates. Following CA/Browser Forum Ballot SC-081v3 — approved in April 2025 — the maximum validity of a public certificate drops from 398 days today to just 47 days by 2029.

Here's the schedule every organisation should plan around:

- Today — 398 days maximum validity
- 15 March 2026 — 200 days maximum validity
- 15 March 2027 — 100 days maximum validity
- 15 March 2029 — 47 days maximum validity

Domain validation reuse windows shrink in step, reaching just 10 days in 2029. In practice, manually renewing certificates once a year will no longer be viable — teams that don't automate face far more frequent renewals and a real risk of costly outages from expired certificates.

The changes apply to publicly-trusted certificates in the Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge trust stores. Internal PKIs are not affected.
