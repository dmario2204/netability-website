On 6 March 2026 MAS issued Consultation Paper P004-2026, proposing Guidelines on Third-Party Risk Management. The consultation closed on 20 April 2026 and the final Guidelines are still pending. When they are issued, the proposed transition period is six months.

Six months. The AI Risk Management Guidelines, still unissued, carry a proposed twelve. This one gives you half as long to do work of a similar shape.

The headline change is scope. The TPRM Guidelines would supersede the Guidelines on Outsourcing for banks and for financial institutions other than banks, and extend expectations beyond outsourcing to all third-party arrangements. Notices 658 and 1121 stay in force for banks and merchant banks. For everyone else, the question stops being "is this outsourcing?" and becomes "what does this third party touch?"

That matters because of what it drags into scope. The SaaS tool a desk licensed on a card. The market data feed. The vendor-managed appliance in your rack. The platform that is plainly not outsourcing under the current definition and so never made the register.

MAS also proposes that firms submit a register of third-party arrangements semi-annually and on request, covering at minimum all material arrangements, including material sub-contractors where possible. Sub-contractors are the part with the longest lead time. You cannot populate that column from your own records. It requires asking each vendor who they depend on, and waiting for an answer.

The rest of the framework is familiar: a formal TPRM framework aligned to operational risk management, board and senior management accountability, and lifecycle controls from risk assessment and due diligence through contracting, monitoring and termination. A parallel consultation would update the Operational Risk Management Guidelines, replacing the 2013 version, also with a six-month transition.

None of this is in force yet. That is the point. The inventory is the long pole, and it is the one piece of work that is entirely within your control today, before the clock starts.
