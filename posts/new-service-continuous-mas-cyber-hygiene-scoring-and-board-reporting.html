New service: continuous MAS Cyber Hygiene scoring and board reporting

Netability Singapore · 24 August 2026

We've launched a dedicated MAS Cyber Hygiene service for financial institutions in Singapore — continuous assessment across all six domains of the Notice, with scored reports ready for management and board review at any time.

The Notice on Cyber Hygiene sets binding requirements in six areas:

  • Administrative accounts — securing every privileged account across systems, databases and network devices
  • Security patches — applying available patches to address vulnerabilities in a timely manner
  • Security standards — establishing and enforcing a baseline security standard
  • Network perimeter defence — restricting unauthorised traffic at the perimeter
  • Malware protection — deploying anti-malware measures on every system where available
  • Multi-factor authentication — MFA on all administrative accounts for critical systems, and on internet-facing accounts that reach customer information

Most institutions we speak to can demonstrate compliance on the day of an assessment. Far fewer can show it held every day in between — and that gap is what surfaces during a review.

Our service maps your environment against each of the six domains, scores your posture continuously rather than annually, and produces the evidence in a format your board and your regulator both accept. Where gaps appear, we remediate them with the same engineers who found them.

If you're a bank, insurer, capital markets or payment services licensee, this applies to you. Talk to us for a scoping review.
