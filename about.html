Our Story

Swiss precision.
Asian market depth.

Netability was founded in 2003 in Lugano, Switzerland — not by a team of business developers, but by two practising engineers with deep roots in network voice, infrastructure and security. That technical DNA has never left us. It runs through every engagement we deliver, every solution we architect, and every team member we hire.

We established our Singapore headquarters in 2006 — our regional centre of competence for Asia-Pacific. From here, we serve financial services institutions, government and regulated agencies, multinational conglomerates, and asset management firms across the region. Our solutions have evolved as the market has: from voice and networking in our early years, to today's mix of cloud, cybersecurity and business compliance.

Our roadmap extends into the Middle East — a reflection of a business that keeps growing, keeps learning, and keeps finding new ways to deliver value to customers who trust us with their most critical systems.
